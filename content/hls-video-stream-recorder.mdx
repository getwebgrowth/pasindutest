## The Business Challenge & Client Pain Points

The client needed a reliable tool to capture educational video lectures and live web streams delivered via segmented HLS protocol. Traditional browser screen recorders degrade quality and drop audio frames, while standard network downloaders fail to reassemble dynamic `.ts` video chunks.

## Core Technical Innovations & Solved Engineering Hurdles

### 1. Declarative Net Request HLS Manifest Sniffing
Utilized `chrome.declarativeNetRequest` and `chrome.webRequest` APIs to passively sniff network request headers, identifying active master `.m3u8` playlists and audio/video transport streams.

### 2. Concurrent Transport Stream (.ts) Chunk Fetcher
Engineered an asynchronous worker queue that downloads sequential video segments in parallel with automatic retries and cryptographic AES-128 segment decryption.

### 3. In-Browser FFmpeg WebAssembly (WASM) Muxing
Integrated `@ffmpeg/ffmpeg` compiled to WebAssembly, muxing raw AAC audio and H.264 video streams directly in the browser without sending data to an external server.

### 4. Offscreen Document Large Blob Streaming
Managed multi-gigabyte video files in Chromium using Offscreen Documents and `showSaveFilePicker` streaming filesystem APIs to prevent browser tab out-of-memory crashes.

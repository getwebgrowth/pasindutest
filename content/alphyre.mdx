## Executive Summary

**Alphyre** is an institutional-grade crypto intelligence dashboard delivering token velocity signals, whale transaction tracking, and automated AI analysis. It features friction-free pay-per-query access using the **X402 HTTP micropayment standard**, allowing users to pay fractions of a cent in USDC directly from their Solana wallet.

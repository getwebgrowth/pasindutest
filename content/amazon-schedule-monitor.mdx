## Executive Summary

**Amazon Schedule Monitor** is a specialized background monitoring Chrome extension designed for flexible workforce employees and logistics staff. It continuously checks for newly opened work blocks, sounds instant audio alerts, and triggers system notifications the second a preferred shift opens.

- **Source Code**: [github.com/pasindupiumal03/Amazon-Schedule-Monitor-Extension](https://github.com/pasindupiumal03/Amazon-Schedule-Monitor-Extension)

---

## Technical Stack

- **Extension Framework**: Manifest V3, `chrome.notifications`, `chrome.alarms`, `chrome.storage`.
- **DOM Engine**: MutationObservers, Dynamic Content Scripts.
